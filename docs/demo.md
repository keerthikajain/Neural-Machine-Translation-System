# 60-second demonstration

1. Open Saarthi on localhost after setup. Enter the Anita, Pune, Mumbai and three-hour example from the README, then click **Translate**.
2. Point out the name, route and duration in the Hindi result. Click **Copy** and show the confirmation.
3. Open **History** to find the result, or choose a **Quick Phrase** to fill the input. Narrow the browser window to show the stacked translation panels and bottom navigation.
4. Open **About the models**. Identify AI4Bharat IndicTrans2 as the pretrained demo backend and Saarthi Compact A as the separately trained experimental model.
5. Show the evaluation table. Mention the decimal failure and explain that readers should check important facts.

If the first translation takes longer, show the loading state. Use a short passage when demonstrating recovery from a length error.
