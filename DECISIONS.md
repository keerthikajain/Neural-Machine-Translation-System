# Saarthi NMT - Project Development Principles

## Student Ownership
- Keerthika understands the architecture, implementation, training process, and technical decisions.
- Explain concepts before introducing complicated implementation changes.
- Break larger tasks into manageable steps for active participation.

## AI-Assisted Development
- Kiro assists with debugging, code suggestions, test generation, research, and reviewing.
- No blind implementation of large features without explaining purpose.
- Important technical decisions and trade-offs are presented before changes.
- Keerthika reviews and approves significant changes.

## Authentic Documentation
- Documentation is natural, clear, and technically accurate.
- Document actual progress, experiments, mistakes, debugging, and decisions.
- Explain why specific architecture, tokenizer, dataset, or training configuration was selected.
- No exaggerated claims, invented results, or generic AI-generated descriptions.

## Technical Ownership
- After implementing important components, explain how they work.
- Provide small verification/testing tasks for Keerthika.
- Prepare for explaining and defending code during OJT review or viva.

## Project Integrity
- Preserve failed experiments and their findings.
- Clearly distinguish implemented, tested, planned, and unverified features.
- Keep Git history and experiment records meaningful.
- Do not hide AI assistance or misrepresent authorship.

## Credit Efficiency
- Follow the credit-efficient training plan (docs/CREDIT_EFFICIENT_PLAN.md).
- Do not waste compute on unnecessary experiments.
- Ask before significant changes or launching paid resources.
- CPU for diagnostics, GPU only for full training after validation gates pass.
